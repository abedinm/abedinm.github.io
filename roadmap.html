What I won't ship.

  • Analytics. Your visit stays your own. No GA. No Plausible. No Fathom.
  • Newsletter pop-up. If you want updates, the RSS feed is right there.
  • Live chat widget. Email me. I'll answer within 24h.
  • "Resources" library. If it's worth writing, it's an essay or a tool. Not gated PDF bait.
  • Logo grid I can't verify. Every logo on the page links to a live client.
  • Auto-playing video. If it's important, it's still important when you press play.
  • Cookie banner. No cookies = no banner.
  • "Trusted by 1000+ companies." 10. The number is 10. I'd rather you know.

# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**This is a working draft.** It's the version I use as a starting point when a client doesn't have one of their own. Both sides can mark it up. Send your redlines and I'll respond same-day.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties intend to discuss and potentially engage in software-engineering work. To do so they will exchange information that is non-public, commercially sensitive, or otherwise confidential ("**Confidential Information**"). This agreement covers how that information is handled.

## 2. What counts as Confidential Information

Anything shared by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") with the other ("Receiving Party") that is:

- marked as confidential, OR
- a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the context, including (but not limited to): source code, repository access, customer lists, financials, pricing, product roadmaps, unpublished press, design files, credentials, and the existence of the engagement itself.

Information is **not** Confidential if:

- it was already public when disclosed, or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party;
- the Receiving Party already had it before disclosure (with provable timeline);
- it was independently developed without reference to the Confidential Information;
- a third party rightfully provided it without a confidentiality obligation.

## 3. What the Receiving Party agrees to

- Use Confidential Information **only** to evaluate or perform the engagement.
- Not disclose it to anyone else without written permission, except to subcontractors / advisors who are themselves bound by an NDA at least as strict as this one.
- Protect it with the same care it would use for its own confidential information (and at minimum, reasonable care).
- Notify the Disclosing Party as soon as practical if any unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure occurs.

## 4. Duration

- Confidentiality obligations apply for **3 years** from the effective date.
- Trade secrets remain protected for as long as they retain trade-secret status under applicable law.

## 5. Return / destruction

On written request, the Receiving Party will return or destroy Confidential Information within **14 days**, except as required by law or for routine backup retention.

## 6. No license, no obligation to engage

Nothing in this agreement grants a license to any intellectual property or obliges either Party to enter into a paid engagement. A separate signed Statement of Work or Master Services Agreement governs any paid work.

## 7. Compelled disclosure

If a court or competent authority compels disclosure of Confidential Information, the Receiving Party may comply *only after* giving the Disclosing Party prompt notice and a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order, where legally permitted.

## 8. Governing law and venue

This agreement is governed by **the laws of the Netherlands**, with venue in the courts of Amsterdam — unless the Parties mutually agree on a different jurisdiction in writing (e.g. UK, Bangladesh, or the Client's home jurisdiction). I'm flexible; pick what's defensible for both sides.

## 9. Entire agreement, severability

This is the entire agreement on confidentiality between the Parties. If any clause is held unenforceable, the rest stays in force. Amendments must be in writing and signed.

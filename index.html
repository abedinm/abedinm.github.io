How it actually ships.

One-pager

You send a one-paragraph scope. I read it, write a one-page spec, send it back within 48 hours.

Plain English. Goals · constraints · what's out of scope. If your scope doesn't fit on one page, the project is too vague to start — we refine until it does. See a real shipped scope: Deltadesh →

+
Step 02

Build, in daylight

One repo, one branch, one engineer. Daily commits you can read. Weekly Loom walkthrough.

You get repo access from day one. Stuck? You hear within 24 hours — usually the same day, often the same hour. No status calls to bill for.

+

Ship + maintain

MVP deployed, signed, documented. Then I stay — retainer or at-cost on demand. I haven't ghosted an engagement yet.
